On the AI 문체 axis in guard turn files, this author's habits split cleanly.

**Not findings (leave them):**

- `~가 아니라 ~다` used for a real technical contrast — `트리거는 권한 모드가 아니라
  모델이다`, `주입원이 파일이 아니라 런타임이다`. These carry the finding itself
  (hypothesis vs. measurement), so several per document is not essay register. Only flag a
  `~가 아니라` whose two halves are rhetorical rather than factual.
- one bolded takeaway sentence per document — see the bold convention in
  [[genre-register-answer-files]].

**Findings (repair them):**

- a metaphor standing in for the plain fact — `내가 깃발을 세우고 사용자가 그것을 집은
  것이다` → `내가 먼저 언급하고 사용자가 그것을 물은 것이다`.
- a one-word metaphorical section heading — `## 사슬` → `## 경위`.
- a 구어체 self-important heading over a `~다` body — `## 남긴 것 — 이게 구별의 핵심이다`
  → `## 남긴 것 — 구별의 핵심`. Trim the clause, do not rewrite the section.

**Why:** counting every `~가 아니라` as 대구 would inflate the AI 문체 count on documents
whose whole point is contrasting a wrong hypothesis with a measured result, and every
finding becomes an edit.

**How to apply:** on the AI 문체 pass, separate contrastive-informative parallels from
decorative ones before counting. See [[terms-left-alone]].
